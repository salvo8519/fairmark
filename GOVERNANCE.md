# Governance

Fairmark is maintained by its founding maintainer, who has final say on scope and methodology
while the project is young. As the community grows, maintainers are added for areas
(`core`, `python`, `docs`, `real-estate`) based on sustained, high-quality contributions.

- Methodology decisions are discussed publicly (Discussions / `needs-methodology` issues) and
  recorded as ADRs in `docs/en/project/adr/`.
- Numerical changes require approval from a maintainer of `crates/fairmark-core`.
- Contact: open a Discussion or use the maintainers' contact on the GitHub organization profile.
