Fairmark now builds the full amortisation schedule of a loan: for every period the instalment, the interest portion, the principal portion and the outstanding balance, with the totals. It covers constant-instalment (French), constant-principal (Italian-style) and bullet loans, an initial interest-only period and a balloon amount. Instalments can be rounded to the cent as banks do: the last instalment then absorbs the rounding so that the balance ends exactly at zero, and the adjustment is reported.
