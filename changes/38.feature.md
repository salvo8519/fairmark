Fairmark now builds the free cash flows of a business plan from its reclassified line items: for every year, EBIT less the taxes on EBIT, plus depreciation and amortisation, less capital expenditure and the increase in net working capital, plus any labelled adjustment, gives the free cash flow to the firm; with interest expense and net borrowing it also gives the free cash flow to equity. Taxes are given as a rate on EBIT or as amounts per year, and the treatment of a negative EBIT is an explicit choice, so every assumption that changes the flows is visible in the result. The DCF can take these line items directly in a stage of given flows, so a business plan can be valued from EBIT to equity value in one call.
