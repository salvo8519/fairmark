The DCF on free cash flows to the firm takes a new input. The single list of flows, discount rate, terminal growth and net debt are replaced by explicit stages, the WACC (one rate, or one per year with a terminal rate), the terms of the flows and of the rates, a required timing, the terminal technique and the net financial position with its components; what was called enterprise value (the present values only) is now the value of operations. The published examples give the same numbers in the new form.
