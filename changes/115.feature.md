Terminal value as a family of explicit methods: stable growth (Gordon), growth earned by reinvestment at a given return on new capital, the H-model for declining growth, exit multiples with the growth they imply, the Italian finite-horizon and normalised-income forms, and liquidation value with every line shown. Each result states its assumptions and whether the growth respects a cap you set, and a comparison runs several techniques on the same rate and basis side by side, so the effect of the choice of technique on the valuation is visible.
