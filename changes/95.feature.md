Fairmark now works with dates and with rates that change over time. You can value dated cash flows from any valuation date (XNPV and XIRR, matching the spreadsheet standard), choose the day-count convention (Actual/365, Actual/360, Actual/Actual ISDA, 30/360, 30E/360) and the compounding, discount each year of a plan at its own rate, and compute MIRR, annuities and perpetuities. Every result states the conventions it used.
