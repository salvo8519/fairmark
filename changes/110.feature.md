The DCF now models a business plan in up to three stages: years of given flows, years of constant growth, and years in which growth declines in equal steps to its long-term rate. Each year shows its flow, growth, rate, discount factor and present value; rates can change every year; a plan in real terms discounted at nominal rates is refused instead of silently mixed; and the result moves from the value of operations to enterprise value and equity value with every item listed.
