Fairmark can now value the equity of a company directly from its free cash flows to equity, discounted at the cost of equity, with the same stages, inflation handling and terminal value choices as the enterprise DCF. A plan can give the flows as amounts or build them from EBIT, taxes, investment, working capital, interest and new borrowing; because the debt is already in these flows, a net financial position is refused, while excess cash, debt-like items and minority interests are added or subtracted with every line shown in the audit trail.
