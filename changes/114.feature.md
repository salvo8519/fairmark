Financial ratios organised by the question they answer: profitability (ROE, ROI, ROA, ROS, margins, capital turnover, and the decomposition of ROE in three factors and in the leverage form with the leverage effect and the effective tax rate), capital structure, liquidity, working capital days and debt service (interest cover, net financial position to EBITDA, DSCR per period and LLCR, with an optional debt service reserve account). Closing or average balances and the 360 or 365-day basis are explicit choices, the net financial position is given with its composition, and a ratio that cannot be computed or is not meaningful, such as ROE with negative equity or a leverage multiple on negative EBITDA, is reported as not defined with the reason while the other ratios are still computed.
