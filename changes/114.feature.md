Financial ratios organised by the question they answer: profitability (ROE, ROI, ROS, margins, capital turnover and the DuPont decomposition of ROE), capital structure, liquidity, working capital days and debt service (interest cover, net financial position to EBITDA, DSCR per period and LLCR). Closing or average balances and the 360 or 365-day basis are explicit choices, the net financial position is given with its composition, and a ratio that cannot be computed, such as ROE with negative equity, is reported as not defined with the reason while the other ratios are still computed.
