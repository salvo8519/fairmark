Every method now reports its status, available or preview, to Python, the command line, the MCP server and function-calling tools, and every result records the status of the method that produced it; a sensitivity table, scenario analysis, goal seek or tornado run on a preview method says so. Preview methods are implemented, tested and documented but not yet independently verified, so an AI agent can tell you when a figure comes from one. The rule for promoting a method to available is published in the release policy and checked automatically. The dates, rates and time value methods (net present value, internal rate of return and related) are preview until their formal code review is completed; their results do not change.
