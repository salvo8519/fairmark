A DCF whose plan is at constant prices can now be discounted at a nominal WACC, and a nominal plan at a real rate, by giving expected inflation, one rate or one per year with a rate for the terminal phase: every discount rate, the terminal one included, is converted to the terms of the plan with the exact Fisher relation, the plan itself is never changed, and every converted rate is shown. Without inflation the mismatch is still refused with an explanation of both fixes, and the perpetual growth is also shown in the other terms, with a warning when a nominal growth is below inflation.
