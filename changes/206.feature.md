Fairmark now builds the periods of a model and the values on them. `dates.schedule` returns the start and end dates of monthly, quarterly, yearly or any other number-of-months periods from a start date, keeping month ends (31 January, 29 February, 31 March) and stating that each period runs from its start date up to, but not including, its end date; `series.project` grows a base value period by period with a constant, linear or per-period growth, converts an annual growth rate to the period length with an explicit choice (compound or proportional), and returns dated values ready for the dated net present value. Interest can now also be compounded, and loan instalments paid, every two or every four months; both new methods are in preview.
