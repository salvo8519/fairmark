The DCF now moves from enterprise value to equity value with every claim stated: debt-like items such as unfunded pension obligations or provisions, and the non-controlling interests (minority interests) in consolidated subsidiaries, are subtracted after the net financial position. Each amount is entered with a label and how it was measured, chosen among book value, market value, fair value and present value, or another basis described in a note; Fairmark subtracts the amount as given, shows every line, its basis and its note in the audit trail, and flags a negative equity value without flooring it at zero.
