The DCF now moves from enterprise value to equity value with every claim stated: debt-like items such as unfunded pension obligations or provisions, and the non-controlling interests (minority interests) in consolidated subsidiaries, are subtracted after the net financial position. Each amount is entered with a label and the basis on which it was measured, for example book value or fair value; Fairmark subtracts the amount as given, shows every line and its basis in the audit trail, and flags a negative equity value without flooring it at zero.
