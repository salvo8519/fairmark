In the free cash flow build-up, when taxes are computed from a rate and a negative EBIT is set to pay no tax (the "zero" choice), the tax saving on interest is now limited to the taxable income available: if EBIT is positive but below the interest expense, interest is deducted only up to EBIT, so total taxes never fall below zero and no tax credit arises on a pre-tax loss. The free cash flow to equity of those years is lower than before (with EBIT 30, interest 50 and a 24% tax rate the tax saving is 7.20 instead of 12, and the flow falls by 4.80); the free cash flow to the firm, the "credit" choice and the DCF on free cash flows to the firm give the same results as before. The build-up is now at version 2.
