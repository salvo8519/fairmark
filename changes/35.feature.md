Fairmark now reorganises a balance sheet into invested capital, non-operating assets, equity, the net financial position and debt-like items, from the statutory aggregates of an Italian filing (ordinary or abridged layout) or from totals a bank has already reclassified, for one or more years. It works with whatever totals are available, lists exactly which inputs are missing for the rest, states every item it takes as zero (subscribed capital unpaid and the "of which" parts not given), checks every total given with no tolerance and only on amounts you gave, and returns the net financial position, the non-operating assets and the current and non-current amounts in the form the DCF and the ratios take directly. The method is in preview and covers the balance sheet.
