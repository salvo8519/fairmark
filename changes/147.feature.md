Covenant tests on the definitions of the loan agreement. For each covenant you give the numerator and the denominator as the agreement defines them, the threshold, whether it is a maximum or a minimum and whether the limit itself is allowed, and the test date. Fairmark returns for each covenant whether it is passed, breached or not tested, the tested value and the headroom, in the unit of the ratio and as a share of the threshold, positive when the covenant is respected and negative when it is breached. A covenant whose ratio cannot be computed, such as one over a zero EBITDA, is reported as not tested with the reason and is never counted as a breach.
