The DCF now accepts every terminal value technique on the enterprise basis, seven in all: stable growth, the H-model, the value driver with return on new capital, an exit multiple of EBITDA, EBIT or sales, a finite horizon with a residual value, a normalised income capitalised at the discount rate of the terminal phase, and a liquidation. The terminal value is the same as the technique computed on its own, its method version is reported, an equity measure (P/E, P/BV, a net income or an equity liquidation) in an enterprise DCF is refused, and a sale price or liquidation value discounted half a year early under mid-year timing is flagged.
